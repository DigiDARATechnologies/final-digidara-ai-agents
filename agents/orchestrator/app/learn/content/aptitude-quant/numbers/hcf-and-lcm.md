## Definitions

- **HCF (GCD)**: the largest number that divides all the given numbers.
- **LCM**: the smallest number that all the given numbers divide.

:::formula
For two numbers a and b: **HCF x LCM = a x b**
:::

## Finding them by prime factors

For 36 and 48: 36 = 2^2 x 3^2, 48 = 2^4 x 3.

- HCF = lowest powers of common primes = 2^2 x 3 = **12**
- LCM = highest powers of all primes = 2^4 x 3^2 = **144**

Check: 12 x 144 = 1728 = 36 x 48.

## When to use which

| Situation | Use |
| --- | --- |
| cut into the **largest equal** pieces, largest tile or rope length | **HCF** |
| events **repeat together**, bells, traffic lights, smallest number divisible by all | **LCM** |

:::example Bells
Three bells ring every 12, 15 and 20 minutes. They ring together at 8 am. When will they ring together again?

**Solution.** LCM(12, 15, 20) = 60 minutes, so at **9 am**.
:::

## Remainder problems

:::example Same remainder
What is the smallest number that leaves remainder 3 when divided by 4, 5 and 6?

**Solution.** LCM(4, 5, 6) = 60, so the number is 60 + 3 = **63**.
:::

:::example Largest divisor with remainders
Find the largest number that divides 62, 132 and 237 leaving remainders 2, 6 and 5.

**Solution.** Subtract the remainders: 60, 126, 232. HCF(60, 126, 232) = **2**.
:::

## A missing number

:::example Use the product rule
The LCM of two numbers is 180 and their HCF is 6. One number is 30. Find the other.

**Solution.** Other = (180 x 6) / 30 = **36**.
:::

## Fractions

- LCM of fractions = LCM of numerators / HCF of denominators
- HCF of fractions = HCF of numerators / LCM of denominators

For 2/3 and 4/9: LCM = LCM(2, 4) / HCF(3, 9) = 4/3, and HCF = HCF(2, 4) / LCM(3, 9) = **2/9**.

:::trick
For two numbers in ratio a : b with HCF h, the numbers are ah and bh, and the LCM is abh (when a and b have no common factor).
:::
