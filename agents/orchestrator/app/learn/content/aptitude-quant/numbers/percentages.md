## The idea

**Percent** means "out of 100". 25% is 25 out of 100, or 0.25, or 1/4. Almost every aptitude topic after this one uses it, so it is worth being quick.

:::formula
**x% of y** = (x × y) / 100

**Percentage change** = (new − old) / old × 100

**Percentage point change** is a plain subtraction of two percentages.
:::

## Fractions you should know by heart

| Fraction | Percent | Fraction | Percent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1/2 | 50% | 1/6 | 16.67% |
| 1/3 | 33.33% | 1/8 | 12.5% |
| 1/4 | 25% | 1/10 | 10% |
| 1/5 | 20% | 1/12 | 8.33% |

:::trick
x% of y equals y% of x. So 8% of 50 is the same as 50% of 8, which is 4. Pick whichever is easier.
:::

## Worked examples

:::example Find a percentage
What is 20% of 450?

**Solution.** 20% = 1/5, and 450 / 5 = **90**.
:::

:::example Percentage increase
A price rises from ₹40 to ₹50. What is the percentage increase?

**Solution.** Change = 50 − 40 = 10. Percentage = 10 / 40 × 100 = **25%**.
:::

:::example Successive changes
A price goes up by 20% and then down by 20%. What is the net change?

**Solution.** Multiply the factors: 1.20 × 0.80 = 0.96. The price is 96% of the original, a **4% decrease**, not zero.
:::

:::example Growth over years
A town has 50,000 people. The population grows 10% every year. What is it after 2 years?

**Solution.** 50,000 × 1.1 × 1.1 = 50,000 × 1.21 = **60,500**.
:::

:::example Working backwards
A student needs 40% to pass. He scores 120 marks and fails by 20 marks. What are the total marks?

**Solution.** Pass mark = 120 + 20 = 140, and that is 40% of the total. Total = 140 / 0.40 = **350**.
:::

## Two-step shortcut for net change

If a value changes by a% and then by b%, the net change is:

:::formula
Net % = a + b + (a × b) / 100
:::

Check with +20% and −20%: 20 − 20 + (20 × −20)/100 = −4%. ✓

:::remember
A 50% increase is **not** undone by a 50% decrease. After +50% you have 150; −50% of that leaves 75, which is 25% below the start.
:::
