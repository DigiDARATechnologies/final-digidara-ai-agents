---
title: Partnership
summary: Share profit fairly using the ratio of investment multiplied by time.
takeaways:
- Profit is shared in the ratio of (investment x time)
- When time is the same, the ratio is just the ratio of investments
- A working partner first takes the agreed salary or commission
---
## The rule

:::formula
Profit share is proportional to **investment x time**.
If everyone invests for the same time, use the ratio of investments.
:::

:::example Same time
A, B and C invest 5000, 7000 and 8000 for one year. The profit is 10,000. Find each share.

**Solution.** Ratio 5 : 7 : 8 (20 parts). One part = 500. A = **2500**, B = **3500**, C = **4000**.
:::

## Different times

:::example Different durations
A invests 6000 for 12 months and B invests 8000 for 9 months. Total profit is 30,000. Find each share.

**Solution.** A : B = 6000 x 12 : 8000 x 9 = 72000 : 72000 = 1 : 1. Each gets **15,000**.
:::

:::example Joining later
A starts a business with 20,000. After 4 months B joins with 30,000. After one year the profit is 14,000. Find B's share.

**Solution.** A: 20000 x 12 = 240000. B: 30000 x 8 = 240000. Ratio 1 : 1, so B gets **7,000**.
:::

## Working partner

:::example Salary first
A and B invest 10,000 and 20,000. A also manages the business for a fee of 10% of the profit. The profit is 15,000. Find what each receives.

**Solution.** A's fee = 10% of 15,000 = 1,500. Remaining 13,500 shared in ratio 1 : 2: A gets 4,500, B gets 9,000. A in total = 1,500 + 4,500 = **6,000**, B = **9,000**.
:::

:::trick
Reduce the investment ratio by dividing by a common number before multiplying by time. It keeps the arithmetic small.
:::
