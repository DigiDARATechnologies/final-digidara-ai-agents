---
title: Problems on ages
summary: Turn age sentences into equations: present, past and future ages and ratios.
takeaways:
- Let the unknown age be x and write every other age in terms of x
- After n years add n to each person; n years ago subtract n
- For ratio problems use x as the multiplier
---
## Method

1. Let the **younger** person's present age be `x` (or use the ratio multiplier).
2. Write the other ages in terms of `x`.
3. Add years for the future, subtract for the past.
4. Form one equation and solve.

:::example Multiples
A father is 3 times as old as his son. After 10 years he will be twice as old. Find their present ages.

**Solution.** Son = x, father = 3x. 3x + 10 = 2(x + 10), so x = 10. Son **10**, father **30**.
:::

:::example Ratio and sum
The ages of two people are in ratio 5 : 7 and their sum is 60. Find the ages.

**Solution.** 12 parts = 60, so 1 part = 5. Ages **25** and **35**.
:::

:::example Ratio after some years
The ratio of ages is 3 : 4. After 6 years it will be 4 : 5. Find the present ages.

**Solution.** (3x + 6) / (4x + 6) = 4/5. So 15x + 30 = 16x + 24, giving x = 6. Ages **18** and **24**.
:::

:::example Past age
Five years ago A was twice as old as B. Now their ages add up to 40. Find A's age.

**Solution.** Let B = b now, A = 40 - b. 40 - b - 5 = 2(b - 5), so 35 - b = 2b - 10, 3b = 45, b = 15. A = **25**.
:::

## Average age

:::formula
Sum of ages = average x number of people. When a person leaves or joins, the **sum** changes.
:::

:::example
The average age of 4 friends is 20. A fifth friend joins and the average becomes 22. How old is the new friend?

**Solution.** New sum = 5 x 22 = 110. Old sum = 4 x 20 = 80. New friend = **30**.
:::

:::trick
The difference between two people's ages **never changes**. Use it to link the past and future.
:::
