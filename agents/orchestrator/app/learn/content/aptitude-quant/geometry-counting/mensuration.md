## 2D shapes

| Shape | Area | Perimeter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rectangle (l, b) | l x b | 2(l + b) |
| Square (a) | a squared | 4a |
| Triangle (base b, height h) | (1/2) x b x h | sum of sides |
| Circle (r) | pi r squared | 2 pi r |
| Parallelogram | base x height | 2(a + b) |
| Trapezium | (1/2)(a + b) x h | sum of sides |

Use pi = 22/7 unless told otherwise.

:::example Circle
A circle has radius 7 cm. Find its area and circumference.

**Solution.** Area = 22/7 x 49 = **154 cm squared**. Circumference = 2 x 22/7 x 7 = **44 cm**.
:::

:::formula
Heron's formula: area = sqrt(s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c)), where s = (a + b + c) / 2
:::

:::example Heron
Triangle with sides 13, 14 and 15 cm.

**Solution.** s = 21. Area = sqrt(21 x 8 x 7 x 6) = sqrt(7056) = **84 cm squared**.
:::

## 3D solids

| Solid | Volume | Surface area |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cube (a) | a cubed | 6 a squared |
| Cuboid (l, b, h) | l x b x h | 2(lb + bh + hl) |
| Cylinder (r, h) | pi r squared h | curved: 2 pi r h; total: 2 pi r (r + h) |
| Cone (r, h, slant l) | (1/3) pi r squared h | curved: pi r l; total: pi r (r + l) |
| Sphere (r) | (4/3) pi r cubed | 4 pi r squared |
| Hemisphere | (2/3) pi r cubed | curved 2 pi r squared; total 3 pi r squared |

:::example Cylinder
Volume of a cylinder with r = 7 cm and h = 10 cm.

**Solution.** 22/7 x 49 x 10 = **1540 cm cubed**.
:::

:::example Cube
A cube has side 5 cm. Surface area and volume?

**Solution.** Surface = 6 x 25 = **150 cm squared**. Volume = **125 cm cubed**.
:::

:::example Painting a room
A room is 5 m long, 4 m wide and 3 m high. Find the area of the four walls.

**Solution.** 2(5 + 4) x 3 = **54 m squared**.
:::

:::example Sphere
Volume of a sphere with radius 3 cm (use pi = 22/7).

**Solution.** (4/3) x 22/7 x 27 = **113.14 cm cubed** (approximately).
:::

## Melting and reshaping

When a solid is melted and recast, the **volume stays the same**.

:::example
A metal sphere of radius 3 cm is melted to make small spheres of radius 1 cm. How many?

**Solution.** Volume ratio = (3/1) cubed = **27 spheres**.
:::

:::trick
If the radius of a circle becomes k times, its area becomes k squared times. If every side of a cube doubles, the volume becomes 8 times.
:::
