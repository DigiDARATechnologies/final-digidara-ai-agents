title: Races and circular tracks
summary: Solve questions on who beats whom, head starts, dead heats and runners meeting on a circular track.
takeaways:
- Compare the distances covered in the same time
- On a circular track, same direction uses the difference of speeds and opposite uses the sum
- Time to meet = track length / relative speed (for the first meeting)
---
## Races

If A beats B by some distance, in the time A finishes, B is that far short.

:::example Beating by distance
A runs 100 m in 20 s and B runs 100 m in 25 s. By how much does A beat B?

**Solution.** A finishes in 20 s. In 20 s B covers 100 / 25 x 20 = 80 m. A wins by **20 metres**.
:::

:::example Beating by time
In the same race, by how much time does A win?

**Solution.** A finishes in 20 s and B in 25 s, so A wins by **5 seconds**.
:::

## Head starts

:::example
A gives B a 20 m start in a 100 m race and they finish together (a dead heat). A runs 100 m while B runs 80 m. What is the ratio of their speeds?

**Solution.** Same time, so speed ratio = distance ratio = 100 : 80 = **5 : 4**.
:::

:::example Whom does the faster win?
A's speed is 5 m/s and B's is 4 m/s. In a 200 m race, by how many metres does A beat B?

**Solution.** A takes 200 / 5 = 40 s. B covers 4 x 40 = 160 m. A beats B by **40 m**.
:::

## Circular track

Two runners start from the same point on a circular track.

:::formula
Same direction: first meeting after  track length / (faster speed - slower speed)
Opposite directions: first meeting after  track length / (sum of speeds)
:::

:::example Same direction
Two runners with speeds 4 m/s and 6 m/s run in the same direction on a 400 m track. When do they first meet?

**Solution.** Relative speed = 6 - 4 = 2 m/s. Time = 400 / 2 = **200 seconds**.
:::

:::example Opposite directions
The same runners run in opposite directions. When do they first meet?

**Solution.** Relative speed = 6 + 4 = 10 m/s. Time = 400 / 10 = **40 seconds**.
:::

## Meeting at the starting point

:::example
Two runners take 12 minutes and 18 minutes to complete one round. After how many minutes will they be together at the starting point again?

**Solution.** The LCM of 12 and 18 is **36 minutes**.
:::

:::trick
For "when do they meet again at the start", use the LCM of their lap times. For "when do they meet anywhere", use the relative speed.
:::
