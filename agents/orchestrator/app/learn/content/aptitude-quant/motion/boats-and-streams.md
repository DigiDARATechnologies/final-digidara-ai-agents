---
title: Boats and streams
summary: Work with downstream and upstream speeds to find the boat's speed in still water and the speed of the stream.
takeaways:
- Downstream speed = boat + stream; upstream speed = boat - stream
- Boat speed = (downstream + upstream) / 2; stream speed = (downstream - upstream) / 2
- Use time = distance / speed with the right speed for each direction
---
## The two speeds

Let the boat's speed in still water be **b** and the stream's speed be **s**.

:::formula
Downstream speed d = b + s
Upstream speed u = b - s
b = (d + u) / 2    s = (d - u) / 2
:::

:::example
A boat goes 15 km/h downstream and 9 km/h upstream. Find the boat speed in still water and the stream speed.

**Solution.** b = (15 + 9)/2 = **12 km/h**, s = (15 - 9)/2 = **3 km/h**.
:::

## Time and distance

:::example
A boat's speed in still water is 8 km/h and the stream flows at 2 km/h. How long to go 30 km downstream, and 30 km upstream?

**Solution.** Downstream speed = 10, time = 30/10 = **3 hours**. Upstream speed = 6, time = 30/6 = **5 hours**.
:::

## Round trips

:::example
A boat's speed in still water is 10 km/h and the stream is 2 km/h. It goes 24 km upstream and returns. Total time?

**Solution.** Upstream speed 8: 24/8 = 3 h. Downstream speed 12: 24/12 = 2 h. Total = **5 hours**.
:::

The average speed of a round trip is **not** 10: it is total distance / total time = 48 / 5 = 9.6 km/h.

## Finding the stream speed

:::example
A man rows 20 km downstream in 2 hours and returns in 4 hours. Find the stream speed.

**Solution.** Downstream = 10 km/h, upstream = 5 km/h. Stream = (10 - 5)/2 = **2.5 km/h**.
:::

:::trick
If the time upstream is n times the time downstream for the same distance, then b : s = (n + 1) : (n - 1).
:::
