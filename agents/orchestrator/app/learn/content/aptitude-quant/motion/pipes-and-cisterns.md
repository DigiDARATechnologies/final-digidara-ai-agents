---
title: Pipes and cisterns
summary: Combine filling pipes and emptying pipes using work-rate fractions, with leaks.
takeaways:
- A pipe's rate is 1 / (hours to fill the tank)
- Filling pipes add rates; emptying pipes or leaks subtract
- Time = 1 / combined rate
---
## It is time and work again

A pipe that fills a tank in 10 hours does **1/10 of the tank per hour**. An emptying pipe works against it.

:::formula
Rate of filling pipe = + 1 / time to fill
Rate of emptying pipe or leak = - 1 / time to empty
Combined rate = sum of rates.  Time = 1 / combined rate
:::

:::example Two filling pipes
Pipe A fills a tank in 10 hours, B in 15 hours. Both open together?

**Solution.** 1/10 + 1/15 = 3/30 + 2/30 = 5/30 = 1/6. Time = **6 hours**.
:::

:::example With an outlet
A third pipe C empties the full tank in 30 hours. All three are open. How long to fill?

**Solution.** 1/6 - 1/30 = 5/30 - 1/30 = 4/30 = 2/15. Time = 15/2 = **7.5 hours**.
:::

## A leak

:::example Find the leak
A pipe fills a tank in 6 hours. Because of a leak it takes 8 hours. How long would the leak take to empty a full tank?

**Solution.** Leak rate = 1/6 - 1/8 = 4/24 - 3/24 = 1/24. It empties the tank in **24 hours**.
:::

## Pipes opened at different times

:::example
A fills a tank in 12 hours, B in 20 hours. A is opened first, and after 3 hours B is also opened. When is the tank full?

**Solution.** A alone in 3 hours: 3/12 = 1/4. Remaining 3/4. Together: 1/12 + 1/20 = 5/60 + 3/60 = 8/60 = 2/15 per hour. Time = (3/4) / (2/15) = 45/8 = 5.625 hours more. Total = 3 + 5.625 = **8.625 hours** (8 hours 37.5 minutes).
:::

:::trick
Take the LCM of the times as the total capacity (units of water) so every rate becomes a whole number. For 10 and 15 hours, take 30 units: rates 3 and 2 per hour, combined 5, time = 30/5 = 6 hours.
:::
