## The three-way formula

:::formula
Speed = Distance / Time

Distance = Speed × Time

Time = Distance / Speed
:::

Keep the units the same. A common slip is mixing km, metres, hours and seconds.

## Converting units

| From | To | Do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| km/h | m/s | × 5/18 |
| m/s | km/h | × 18/5 |

54 km/h = 54 × 5/18 = **15 m/s**. And 20 m/s = 20 × 18/5 = **72 km/h**.

:::example Basic
How long does a 180 km journey take at 60 km/h?

**Solution.** Time = 180 / 60 = **3 hours**.
:::

## Average speed

For equal distances at speeds x and y, average speed = 2xy / (x + y).

:::example Equal distances
A man goes to a place at 30 km/h and returns at 60 km/h. What is his average speed?

**Solution.** 2 × 30 × 60 / 90 = **40 km/h**.
:::

## Relative speed

| Situation | Relative speed |
| --- | --- |
| Moving towards each other (opposite directions) | add the speeds |
| Moving the same way | subtract the speeds |

:::example Meeting
Two towns are 300 km apart. Two cars start together towards each other at 40 km/h and 60 km/h. When do they meet?

**Solution.** Closing speed = 100 km/h. Time = 300 / 100 = **3 hours**.
:::

## Trains

A train must travel its **own length** as well as the object's length to pass it completely.

:::example Passing a pole
A 150 m train runs at 54 km/h. How long does it take to pass a pole?

**Solution.** Speed = 15 m/s. Time = 150 / 15 = **10 seconds**.
:::

:::example Crossing a platform
The same train crosses a 150 m platform. How long does it take?

**Solution.** Distance = 150 + 150 = 300 m. Time = 300 / 15 = **20 seconds**.
:::

:::example Two trains crossing
Two trains 120 m and 180 m long run in opposite directions at 50 km/h and 40 km/h. How long do they take to cross each other?

**Solution.** Relative speed = 90 km/h = 25 m/s. Total length = 300 m. Time = 300 / 25 = **12 seconds**.
:::

## Boats and streams

| Term | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| Downstream speed | boat + stream |
| Upstream speed | boat − stream |
| Boat in still water | (downstream + upstream) / 2 |
| Stream speed | (downstream − upstream) / 2 |

:::example Boat
A boat goes at 12 km/h in still water and the stream flows at 3 km/h. How long does it take to go 45 km downstream and come back?

**Solution.** Downstream = 15 km/h, so 45 / 15 = 3 h. Upstream = 9 km/h, so 45 / 9 = 5 h. Total = **8 hours**.
:::

:::trick
Speeds in a ratio a : b mean times (for the same distance) in the ratio b : a. If A is twice as fast as B, A takes half the time.
:::
