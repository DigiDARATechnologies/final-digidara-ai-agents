- Give a real weakness and say what you are doing about it
- Be honest and flexible on salary, location and shifts

### Q [Easy] What are your strengths?
Choose **two** that fit the job and prove each with one example.

*Sample:* "I learn quickly. In my final-year project I had never used Flask, and I had a working login system within a week. I am also reliable: I have never missed a project deadline."

### Q [Medium] What is your weakness?
Be honest, choose something real but not fatal for the job, and show you are working on it.

*Sample:* "I used to take on too much myself instead of asking for help. Now I set a rule: if I am stuck for an hour, I ask a teammate. It has made me faster."

Avoid "I am a perfectionist" and "I have no weaknesses". Interviewers have heard both many times.

### Q [Medium] Where do you see yourself in five years?
They want to know you are ambitious but realistic, and likely to stay.

*Sample:* "In five years I would like to be a confident developer who can lead a small module and help newer teammates. I would like to keep growing here."

Do not say "I want your job" or "I will start my own business" in a first interview.

### Q [Medium] Are you willing to relocate or work in shifts?
If you are flexible, say so clearly. If you have a real limit, say it politely and give a reason. Never say yes and then refuse later.

### Q [Medium] What are your salary expectations?
If you can, ask first: "I would like to understand the role and the pay structure. As a fresher I expect what is standard for this position." If pushed for a number, give a **range** based on research of similar roles.

### Q [Medium] You have a gap in your studies. Why?
Say the true reason briefly (health, family, preparing for exams, upskilling), and then talk about what you did or learned, and that you are ready now. Stay calm and do not apologise too much.

### Q [Medium] Why did you leave your last job or internship?
Stay positive. Talk about what you want next (more learning, a larger project), not about complaints about the old team or manager.

### Q [Hard] What makes you different from other candidates?
Do not compare yourself with others. Name one combination of skills or experiences that is hard to find, and give a proof point.

*Sample:* "I have built projects and I have also explained them in college workshops. So I can both build and communicate, which helps in client-facing teams."

:::interview
**Tip:** "weakness" answers are judged on **self-awareness** and the **fix**. A weakness with no plan sounds careless, and a fake weakness sounds dishonest.
:::
