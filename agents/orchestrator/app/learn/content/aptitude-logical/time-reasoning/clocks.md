## How the hands move

- The **minute hand** moves 360 degrees in 60 minutes: **6 degrees per minute**.
- The **hour hand** moves 30 degrees per hour: **0.5 degrees per minute**.
- So the minute hand gains **5.5 degrees per minute** on the hour hand.

:::formula
Angle between the hands at H hours M minutes = | 30H - 5.5M |
If the result is more than 180, take 360 minus it.
:::

:::example Angle at 3:40
**Solution.** |30 x 3 - 5.5 x 40| = |90 - 220| = 130. The angle is **130 degrees**.
:::

:::example Angle at 4:20
**Solution.** |120 - 110| = **10 degrees**.
:::

:::example Angle at 7:00
**Solution.** |210 - 0| = 210, which is more than 180, so 360 - 210 = **150 degrees**.
:::

## Overlapping and right angles

In 12 hours:
- the hands **overlap 11 times** (22 times in a day),
- they point in **opposite** directions (180 degrees) 11 times,
- they form a **right angle 22 times** (44 times in a day).

:::formula
The hands overlap after H o'clock at (60H / 11) minutes past H.
:::

:::example When do they overlap after 4?
**Solution.** 60 x 4 / 11 = 240/11 = 21 9/11 minutes. So at **4:21 9/11**.
:::

## Mirror and wrong clocks

:::trick
Mirror image of a clock time: subtract the time from **11:60** (that is, 12:00). The mirror image of 3:25 is 11:60 - 3:25 = **8:35**.
:::

:::example A slow clock
A clock loses 5 minutes every hour. It is set right at 12 noon. What time will it show after the real time is 6 pm?

**Solution.** In 6 real hours it loses 6 x 5 = 30 minutes, so it shows **5:30 pm**.
:::

:::remember
The angle at exactly H o'clock is 30H degrees. Use the formula and always check whether the answer exceeds 180.
:::
