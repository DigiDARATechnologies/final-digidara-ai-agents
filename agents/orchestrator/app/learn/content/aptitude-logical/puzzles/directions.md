## The compass

North is up, east is right, south is down, west is left. Turning **right** moves you clockwise: north → east → south → west → north. Turning **left** goes the other way.

| Facing | Turn right | Turn left |
| --- | --- | --- |
| North | East | West |
| East | South | North |
| South | West | East |
| West | North | South |

:::example Following the turns
Ravi faces east. He turns left, then left again, then right. Which way does he face now?

**Solution.** East → left → North → left → West → right → **North**.
:::

## Distance from the start

Draw the path with a pencil. Each turn is a right angle.

:::example A right angle
A walks 5 km north, then turns right and walks 12 km. How far is A from the start?

**Solution.** After turning right from north, A walks east. The two legs are the sides of a right-angled triangle: √(5² + 12²) = √169 = **13 km**.
:::

:::example Coming back
A walks 10 m south, then 6 m west, then 10 m north. Where is A relative to the start?

**Solution.** The north and south legs cancel. A is **6 m west** of the start.
:::

## Common right triangles

| Sides | Hypotenuse |
| --- | --- |
| 3, 4 | 5 |
| 5, 12 | 13 |
| 8, 15 | 17 |
| 7, 24 | 25 |

So 3 km east then 4 km north is exactly 5 km from the start. These are worth remembering.

## Shadows

The sun rises in the east and sets in the west. So:

- **Morning:** shadows fall towards the **west**.
- **Evening:** shadows fall towards the **east**.
- At noon the shadow is at its shortest.

:::example A shadow question
In the morning, a man's shadow falls on his right. Which way is he facing?

**Solution.** In the morning the shadow points west. If west is on his right, he faces **south** (facing south, right is west).
:::

## Eight directions

Between the four main directions sit north-east, south-east, south-west and north-west. A turn of 45° moves you to the next of the eight.

:::trick
For long chains, keep a running position as (east-west, north-south), for example "6 west, 0 north". Add or subtract each move, then use Pythagoras once at the end.
:::

:::remember
A left turn from facing south is **east**, not west. Practise the table until it is automatic.
:::
