---
title: Ranking and order
summary: Find positions from the left and right, count people in between, and order people by comparing them.
takeaways:
- Total = position from one end + position from the other end - 1
- People between two positions = difference of positions - 1
- Convert comparisons into a single ordered line
---
## The key formula

:::formula
Total = (position from left) + (position from right) - 1
People between two positions = (larger position - smaller position) - 1
:::

:::example Total in the class
Ravi is 12th from the top and 20th from the bottom in a class. How many students are there?

**Solution.** 12 + 20 - 1 = **31**.
:::

:::example Position from the other end
In a row of 40 students Asha is 15th from the left. What is her position from the right?

**Solution.** 40 - 15 + 1 = **26th** from the right.
:::

:::example People in between
Priya is 8th from the top and Neha is 21st from the top. How many students are between them?

**Solution.** 21 - 8 - 1 = **12**.
:::

## Interchange of positions

:::example Swapping
Anil is 7th from the left and Binu is 12th from the right. After they swap places, Anil is 22nd from the left. How many people are in the row?

**Solution.** After the swap Anil stands where Binu stood, which is 22nd from the left. Binu's place is 12th from the right, so total = 22 + 12 - 1 = **33**.
:::

## Ordering by comparison

Turn statements into symbols and chain them.

:::example Heights
P is taller than Q. Q is taller than R. S is shorter than R. Who is the shortest and who is the tallest?

**Solution.** P > Q > R > S. The tallest is **P** and the shortest is **S**.
:::

:::example Ranks
In a test, Kiran scored more than Lata but less than Mona. Nisha scored less than Lata. Arrange from the highest.

**Solution.** Mona > Kiran > Lata > Nisha.
:::

## Class rank problems

:::example Top and bottom
In a class of 45, Sita ranks 14th from the top. What is her rank from the bottom?

**Solution.** 45 - 14 + 1 = **32nd**.
:::

:::trick
If two positions are given from opposite ends and the total is asked, add and subtract 1. If they overlap (the sum is more than the total), the ends cross over.
:::
