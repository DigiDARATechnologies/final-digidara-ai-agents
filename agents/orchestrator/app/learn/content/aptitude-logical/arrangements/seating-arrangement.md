title: Seating arrangement
summary: Solve linear and circular seating puzzles by drawing a diagram and placing the sure facts first.
takeaways:
- Draw seats first, then fill in the facts one by one
- Place the most definite clues (fixed positions, opposites) before the vague ones
- Facing the centre, your left is clockwise and your right is anticlockwise
---
## Method

1. **Draw** a row of boxes (linear) or a circle (circular).
2. Mark the **direction** the people face (north in a row, or the centre of the circle).
3. Place the **fixed** facts first: extreme ends, "exactly in the middle", "opposite to".
4. Add relative facts ("next to", "between"), and use **elimination**.
5. Recheck every clue against the final picture.

## Linear arrangement

Left and right are measured by the person facing **north**: left is the left side of the page for everyone in the row.

:::example Row of five
Five people P, Q, R, S and T sit in a row facing north. P sits at the extreme right. Q sits second from the left. R sits immediately to the right of Q. S does not sit next to P. Who sits in the middle, and who sits at the left end?

**Solution.** Positions 1 to 5 from the left.
- P is at 5. Q is at 2. R is immediately right of Q, so R is at 3.
- Remaining seats: 1 and 4 for S and T. Seat 4 is next to P, and S may not sit next to P, so S is at **1** and T at 4.

Order: **S Q R T P**. The person in the middle is **R**, and the left end is **S**.
:::

## Circular arrangement

When people sit around a table **facing the centre**:

- the **immediate left** of a person is the next one **clockwise**,
- the **immediate right** is the next one **anticlockwise**,
- **opposite** persons are half a circle apart (in 6 seats, seat 1 is opposite seat 4).

:::example Round table
Six friends A, B, C, D, E and F sit around a circular table facing the centre. B sits immediately to the left of A. D sits opposite A. C sits between B and D. E sits immediately to the right of A. Who sits opposite B?

**Solution.** Number the seats clockwise 1 to 6 with A at 1.
- B is immediate left of A, so clockwise: B at 2.
- D opposite A: D at 4. C is between B and D: C at 3.
- E is immediate right of A (anticlockwise): E at 6.
- F takes the last seat, 5.

Clockwise order: A, B, C, D, F, E. Opposite of seat 2 is seat 5, which is **F**.
:::

## When people face outwards

If everyone faces **away** from the centre, left and right swap: the immediate left is now anticlockwise. Read the question for the direction before placing anyone.

## Two rows facing each other

In a double-row puzzle, the people in one row face north and in the other face south, so their left and right are opposite. Draw both rows with arrows and be careful.

:::trick
If a clue has two possible placements, hold both in pencil and let the next clue eliminate one. Never guess early.
:::
