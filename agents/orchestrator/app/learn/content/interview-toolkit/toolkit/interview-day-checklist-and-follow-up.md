- Prepare documents, tech and your route the day before
- Arrive early, be polite to everyone and listen carefully
- Follow up with a short thank-you message

## The day before

- Re-read the **job description**, your resume and your prep sheet.
- Practise answers aloud: introduction, projects, strengths, gaps.
- Prepare **documents**: resumes (printed copies), ID, marksheets, certificates, offer or call letter, pen and notebook.
- Choose and iron your **clothes**: neat, formal or smart casual to match the company.
- Check the **location** and travel time, or test your online setup.
- **Sleep well.**

## Online interview setup

- Test internet, camera, microphone, charger and the meeting link; join **5 to 10 minutes early**.
- Sit in a quiet, well-lit place with a plain background; face the light.
- Close other apps and silence notifications.
- Look at the **camera** when you speak, sit straight and keep paper and a glass of water nearby.
- Have your resume and a notepad ready; if your internet fails, rejoin and apologise briefly.

## On the day (in person)

- Reach **15 to 20 minutes early**; be polite to the staff you meet.
- Switch your phone to silent.
- Greet with a smile and a firm handshake if offered; wait to be asked to sit.
- Keep a calm posture, make **eye contact**, and listen to the whole question before answering.
- Keep answers **clear and structured**; do not ramble.
- Be honest, positive and professional.

## During the interview

- Start with a strong, short introduction.
- Use examples with STAR for behavioural questions.
- If you do not understand, ask the interviewer to repeat or clarify.
- For technical questions, **think aloud** so they follow your reasoning.
- Ask 2 or 3 good questions at the end.
- Thank them before leaving.

## After the interview

Send a short **thank-you message** within 24 hours.

:::example Thank-you email template
**Subject:** Thank you for the interview

Dear Ms. Rao,

Thank you for taking the time to speak with me today about the Junior Developer role. I enjoyed learning about your team's work on the learning platform, and our discussion made me even more interested in the opportunity.

I am confident that my Python and Flask project experience would help me contribute quickly. Please let me know if you need any further information from me.

Thank you again for your time.

Regards,
Asha Kumar
Phone: 98xxxxxxxx
:::

- Note the questions you found hard and **study them**, whatever the result.
- If you do not hear back after the stated time, send **one** polite follow-up.
- If rejected, ask for feedback politely and keep improving.

## Quick checklist

- resumes, ID and certificates ready
- company and role researched
- introduction and project stories practised
- questions prepared to ask
- clothes, route and time planned (or tech tested)
- phone silent, calm mind, good sleep

:::practice mock-interview
Do a full mock interview the day before. It is the best way to reduce nerves.
:::
