## Full stop, question mark, exclamation mark

End a statement with a **full stop (.)**, a question with a **question mark (?)** and strong feeling with an **exclamation mark (!)**.

## Comma

Use a comma:

- in a **list**: "We bought pens, notebooks and files."
- after an **introductory phrase**: "After the exam, we went home."
- to separate **extra information**: "My brother, who lives in Madurai, is a doctor."
- before a conjunction joining **two full sentences**: "I wanted to go, but it rained."

Do **not** put a comma between the subject and its verb: "The students of this class, are bright" is wrong.

Compare: "Let's eat, Grandma." (inviting) and "Let's eat Grandma." (alarming).

## Apostrophe

| Use | Example |
| --- | --- |
| **Belonging** (singular) | the student's book |
| **Belonging** (plural ending in s) | the students' books |
| **Missing letters** | don't (do not), it's (it is), I'm (I am) |

**Not for plurals**: "apple's for sale" is wrong; write "apples for sale". **its** (belonging to it) has no apostrophe: "The dog wagged its tail."

## Semicolon and colon

- **Semicolon (;)** joins two closely related full sentences: "I was tired; I went to bed early."
- **Colon (:)** introduces a list, an explanation or a quotation: "Bring three things: a pen, a ruler and a calculator."

## Other marks

- **Hyphen (-)** joins words: well-known, twenty-five, mother-in-law.
- **Quotation marks** show exact speech: She said, "I am ready."
- **Brackets** hold extra information (like this).

:::example Correct the punctuation
"my sisters friend dont like coffee"

**Solution.** "My sister's friend doesn't like coffee." (capital letter, apostrophe for belonging, correct verb, full stop)
:::

## Commonly misspelt words

| Correct | Common mistake |
| --- | --- |
| **accommodation** | accomodation |
| **definitely** | definately |
| **separate** | seperate |
| **occurrence** | occurence |
| **necessary** | neccessary |
| **receive** | recieve (rule: i before e, except after c) |
| **environment** | enviroment |
| **government** | goverment |
| **embarrass** | embarass |
| **maintenance** | maintainance |
| **independent** | independant |
| **argument** | arguement |
| **calendar** | calender |
| **privilege** | priviledge |
| **recommend** | recomend |
| **conscience** | consience |
| **success** | sucess |
| **tomorrow** | tommorow |

:::trick
Memory aids: there is **a rat** in sep**a**rate; **one c, two s** in necessary (one collar, two sleeves); "**i** before **e**, except after **c**" (receive, ceiling).
:::
