## How to use this list

Learn 10 words a day. Cover the meaning column and test yourself; then cover the word column.

## Positive words

| Word | Meaning | Sentence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Adept** | skilled | She is adept at solving puzzles. |
| **Amiable** | friendly | an amiable colleague |
| **Astute** | clever, sharp | an astute investor |
| **Candid** | honest, open | a candid opinion |
| **Diligent** | hardworking | a diligent student |
| **Eloquent** | speaking well | an eloquent speech |
| **Judicious** | wise, sensible | a judicious decision |
| **Lucid** | clear | a lucid explanation |
| **Meticulous** | very careful | meticulous planning |
| **Resilient** | able to recover quickly | resilient after failure |
| **Serene** | calm | a serene lake |
| **Tenacious** | persistent | a tenacious worker |
| **Zealous** | enthusiastic | a zealous volunteer |

## Action and change words

| Word | Meaning | Sentence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Alleviate** | make less severe | to alleviate pain |
| **Augment** | increase | to augment income |
| **Curtail** | reduce, cut short | to curtail expenses |
| **Deter** | discourage | fines deter littering |
| **Elucidate** | explain clearly | please elucidate the point |
| **Exacerbate** | make worse | the delay exacerbated the problem |
| **Foster** | encourage the growth of | to foster talent |
| **Hinder** | obstruct | rain hindered the match |
| **Mitigate** | lessen the effect | to mitigate risk |
| **Procrastinate** | delay again and again | do not procrastinate |
| **Scrutinise** | examine closely | scrutinise the contract |
| **Substantiate** | prove with evidence | substantiate your claim |
| **Underscore** | emphasise | the report underscores the need |

## Revision plan

- Day 1 to 3: learn 30 words.
- Day 4: revise all 30 and write a sentence for each.
- Repeat with a new set of 30 every four days.
- Revise old sets once a week.

:::tip
Read an English newspaper editorial every day. Seeing words in context makes them stick far better than lists alone.
:::
