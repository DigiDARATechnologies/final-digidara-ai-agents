- Read the sentence slowly, then check the verb, pronoun, modifier and parallelism
- Choose the shortest correct option that keeps the meaning

## A checklist for each sentence

1. **Subject-verb agreement** (previous lessons)
2. **Tense** consistency
3. **Pronoun** agreement and clarity
4. **Parallel structure**
5. **Modifiers** placed next to what they describe
6. **Word choice** (confusing pairs)
7. **Redundancy** (saying the same thing twice)

## 1. Pronoun errors

- A pronoun must match its noun in number: "Each student must bring **his or her** book" (or "their" in modern usage; follow the exam's style).
- Use the right case: "Between you and **me**" (not "I"). "He is taller than **I** (am)."
- Make clear who the pronoun means: "Ravi told Amit that **he** had won" (who?). Rewrite.

## 2. Parallel structure

Items in a list should have the same form.

- Wrong: She likes **to dance**, **singing** and **to paint**.
- Right: She likes **dancing**, **singing** and **painting**.

## 3. Misplaced modifiers

A descriptive phrase must sit next to the word it describes.

- Wrong: **Walking down the road**, the trees looked lovely. (the trees were not walking)
- Right: **Walking down the road**, I thought the trees looked lovely.

## 4. Comparisons

- Wrong: This book is **more better** than that. Right: This book is **better**.
- Compare like with like: "The population of Chennai is larger than **that of** Madurai."
- Use **-er/than** for two, **-est/the** for more than two. "He is the **taller** of the two boys" and "the **tallest** in the class."

## 5. Redundancy

- Wrong: "return **back**", "**repeat again**", "**reason is because**", "**very unique**"
- Right: "return", "repeat", "the reason is that", "unique"

## 6. Confusing words

| Pair | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **its** / **it's** | its = belonging to it; it's = it is |
| **their / there / they're** | their (belonging), there (place), they're (they are) |
| **who / whom** | who = subject (who called?), whom = object (to whom?) |
| **fewer / less** | fewer for countable (fewer books), less for uncountable (less water) |
| **among / between** | between two, among more than two |
| **lay / lie** | lay = put something; lie = rest |

## 7. Other frequent errors

- **Double negatives**: "I don't know nothing" should be "I don't know anything."
- **Wrong preposition**: "He is senior **than** me" should be "senior **to** me".
- **Uncountable nouns made plural**: informations, advices, furnitures, luggages, equipments (use information, advice, furniture, luggage, equipment).

:::example Find the error
"He gave me an advice about the problem."

**Solution.** "Advice" is uncountable, so no "an". Correct: "He gave me **some advice** about the problem."
:::

:::example Choose the best
The manager, as well as his assistants, ____ present at the meeting.
(a) were (b) are (c) was (d) have been

**Solution.** The subject is "manager" (singular); "as well as his assistants" is extra. Correct: **(c) was**.
:::

:::trick
If the sentence sounds fine and you cannot see an error, check **(1) the verb with its subject, (2) the tense, (3) the preposition**. Those cause most hidden errors. If still nothing is wrong, "no error" is the answer.
:::
