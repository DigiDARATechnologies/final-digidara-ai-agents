takeaways:
- Passive voice puts the receiver of the action first: be + past participle
- Keep the same tense when changing voice
- In reported speech move the tense back and change pronouns and time words

## Active and passive voice

- **Active**: the doer comes first. "Ravi **wrote** the letter."
- **Passive**: the receiver comes first. "The letter **was written** by Ravi."

Formula: **object + be (in the same tense) + past participle + by + doer**.

| Tense | Active | Passive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Simple present | She writes a letter. | A letter **is written** by her. |
| Present continuous | She is writing a letter. | A letter **is being written** by her. |
| Present perfect | She has written a letter. | A letter **has been written** by her. |
| Simple past | She wrote a letter. | A letter **was written** by her. |
| Past continuous | She was writing a letter. | A letter **was being written** by her. |
| Simple future | She will write a letter. | A letter **will be written** by her. |
| Modal | She can write a letter. | A letter **can be written** by her. |

Use passive when the doer is unknown or unimportant ("My bike was stolen") or in formal writing.

:::example
Change to passive: "The committee will announce the results tomorrow."

**Solution.** "The results **will be announced** by the committee tomorrow."
:::

## Direct and indirect (reported) speech

- **Direct**: exact words in quotes. He said, "I am tired."
- **Indirect**: reports the idea. He said that he was tired.

### Rules when the reporting verb is in the past

1. Remove the quotes and add **that**.
2. Move the tense **one step back**.
3. Change the **pronouns** to fit the speaker and listener.
4. Change **time and place words**.

| Direct | Indirect |
| --- | --- |
| am, is | was |
| are | were |
| will | would |
| can | could |
| simple present (write) | simple past (wrote) |
| simple past (wrote) | past perfect (had written) |
| present perfect (has written) | past perfect (had written) |

| Direct word | Indirect word |
| --- | --- |
| now | then |
| today | that day |
| tomorrow | the next day |
| yesterday | the previous day |
| here | there |
| this | that |

:::example Statement
Direct: Meena said, "I will call you tomorrow."

**Solution.** Indirect: Meena said that **she would call me the next day**.
:::

### Questions and commands

- **Question**: use **if / whether** for yes-no questions, keep the question word for others, and use normal word order.
 - He asked, "Are you ready?" becomes He asked **if I was ready**.
 - She asked, "Where do you live?" becomes She asked **where I lived**.
- **Command**: use **to**.
 - The teacher said, "Open your books." becomes The teacher **told us to open our books**.

:::remember
Universal truths do not change tense: He said, "The earth is round" becomes He said that the earth **is** round.
:::
