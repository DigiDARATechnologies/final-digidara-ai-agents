title: Articles, prepositions and conjunctions
summary: Choose a, an, the or no article; use common prepositions correctly; and join clauses with the right conjunction.
takeaways:
- A and an are for general single nouns; the points to something specific
- Prepositions of time and place follow fixed patterns
- Conjunctions must match the meaning: contrast, reason, result, condition
---
## Articles

| Article | Use | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **a** | one general thing, next word starts with a **consonant sound** | a book, a university (yoo sound), a one-way street |
| **an** | one general thing, next word starts with a **vowel sound** | an apple, an hour (silent h), an MBA |
| **the** | a specific or known thing; unique things | the sun, the Ganga, the book I lent you |
| **no article** | plural general, uncountable general, most names | Dogs bark. Water is life. India is big. |

Go by the **sound**, not the spelling: **an** honest man, **a** European country.

Use **the** with rivers, seas, mountain ranges, ordinals (the first), superlatives (the best), and musical instruments (play the guitar). Skip it before meals and languages (have lunch, speak Tamil).

## Prepositions

### Time

- **at** a clock time or point: at 5 pm, at night, at noon
- **on** a day or date: on Monday, on 15 August
- **in** months, years, seasons, longer periods: in May, in 2025, in summer

### Place

- **at** a point: at the bus stop
- **on** a surface: on the table
- **in** an enclosed space: in the room, in Chennai

### Common fixed pairs

| Word | Preposition | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| good, bad, weak | **at** | good at maths |
| afraid, fond, proud | **of** | afraid of dogs |
| interested | **in** | interested in music |
| depend, rely | **on** | depends on you |
| differ, different | **from** | different from mine |
| angry | **with** (a person), **at** (a thing) | angry with him |
| married, engaged | **to** | married to her |
| capable | **of** | capable of winning |
| congratulate | **on** | congratulated on his success |
| prefer | **to** | prefer tea to coffee |
| discuss | (no preposition) | discuss the plan |

## Conjunctions

| Meaning | Words | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Addition | and, as well as | He sings and dances. |
| Contrast | but, yet, although, though, whereas | Although it rained, we went out. |
| Reason | because, since, as | I stayed home because I was ill. |
| Result | so, therefore, hence | It was late, so we left. |
| Condition | if, unless, provided that | Unless you hurry, you will miss it. |
| Choice | or, either...or, neither...nor | Tea or coffee? |
| Time | when, while, after, before, until | Wait until I return. |

**Be careful:** do not use two contrast words together. Wrong: "**Although** he was tired, **but** he worked." Right: "Although he was tired, he worked."

:::example Fill the blank
She is fond ____ classical music.
(a) in (b) of (c) at (d) for

**Solution.** "Fond of" is the fixed pair: **(b) of**.
:::

:::example Find the error
"Though he is poor, but he is honest."

**Solution.** "Though" and "but" both show contrast; use only one. Correct: "Though he is poor, he is honest."
:::
