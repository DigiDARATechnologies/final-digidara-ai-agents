title: Fill in the blanks and cloze tests
summary: Choose the right word for a blank using meaning, grammar and clues from the rest of the sentence.
takeaways:
- Read the whole sentence and decide what kind of word fits
- Look for clue words: contrast, cause, result, similarity
- Check the grammar of the option, then the meaning

## Single blank

Steps:

1. Read the sentence and **predict** the word before looking at the options.
2. Check the **grammar** (singular or plural, tense, part of speech).
3. Check the **meaning** and the **tone** (positive or negative).

:::example
Despite his ____ efforts, he failed to win the race.
(a) lazy (b) tireless (c) careless (d) weak

**Solution.** "Despite" shows contrast: he failed even though his efforts were good. **(b) tireless** fits.
:::

## Clue words

| Clue | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **but, however, although, despite, yet** | the blank is the **opposite** of the other part |
| **and, also, moreover** | the blank is **similar** to the other part |
| **because, since** | a **reason** |
| **so, therefore, hence** | a **result** |
| **not only ... but also** | both parts say similar things |

:::example Contrast
The speaker was ____, but the audience was bored.
(a) dull (b) energetic (c) tired (d) late

**Solution.** "But" signals contrast, and the audience was bored, so the speaker must have seemed lively: **(b) energetic**.
:::

## Double blanks

Test each blank on its own, then see which **pair** is consistent in tone.

:::example
The scientist's theory was ____ by experiments, so it was finally ____ by the academic community.
(a) disproved ... accepted (b) confirmed ... accepted (c) confirmed ... rejected (d) disproved ... praised

**Solution.** Test the blanks together. If experiments confirmed the theory, it makes sense that the community accepted it: **(b) confirmed ... accepted**. Options (a) and (d) start with "disproved", which does not fit "so it was finally ...", and (c) is illogical.
:::

## Cloze test (passage with blanks)

A paragraph has 5 to 10 blanks. Tips:

- **Read the whole passage first**, ignoring the blanks, to learn the topic.
- Fill the **easy** blanks (grammar, prepositions) first.
- Then use the **meaning** of the passage for vocabulary blanks.
- Re-read the completed passage to check that it makes sense.

:::example
Regular exercise is ____ for good health. It strengthens the heart, ____ stress and improves sleep.
(1) harmful / essential / optional  (2) increases / reduces / ignores

**Solution.** Blank 1: **essential** (positive, matches the benefits). Blank 2: **reduces** (stress is reduced).
:::

:::tip
If two options seem right, check **collocations** (words that go together): "make a decision" not "do a decision"; "heavy rain" not "strong rain".
:::
