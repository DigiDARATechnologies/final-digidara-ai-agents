Target: 12 minutes.

:::example Q1. Series
2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?

**Solution.** The differences are 4, 6, 8, 10, so the next difference is 12: 30 + 12 = **42**. (These are n x (n + 1).)
:::

:::example Q2. Coding
If CAT is written as 3120 (the positions of C, A and T joined together), how is DOG written?

**Solution.** D = 4, O = 15, G = 7, so **4157**.
:::

:::example Q3. Blood relation
Pointing to a man, Asha said, "He is the son of my mother's only son." How is the man related to Asha?

**Solution.** Asha's mother's only son is Asha's brother. The man is that brother's son, so he is Asha's **nephew**.
:::

:::example Q4. Direction
Ravi walks 5 km north, then 3 km east, then 5 km south. How far is he from the start and in which direction?

**Solution.** The north and south legs cancel, leaving 3 km east: **3 km east**.
:::

:::example Q5. Syllogism
Statements: All cats are animals. All animals are living things.
Conclusion: All cats are living things.

**Solution.** Cats are inside animals, and animals are inside living things, so the conclusion **follows**.
:::

:::example Q6. Ranking
In a class of 25, Kiran is 10th from the top. What is his rank from the bottom?

**Solution.** 25 - 10 + 1 = **16th**.
:::

:::example Q7. Calendar
If 1 January 2026 is a Thursday, what day is 1 March 2026?

**Solution.** January and February 2026 have 31 + 28 = 59 days. 59 divided by 7 leaves 3. Thursday + 3 = **Sunday**.
:::

:::example Q8. Odd one out
3, 5, 7, 9, 11. Which is the odd one out?

**Solution.** All are odd, but 3, 5, 7 and 11 are prime. **9** is not prime (3 x 3).
:::

:::example Q9. Analogy
Doctor : Hospital :: Teacher : ?

**Solution.** A doctor works in a hospital, a teacher works in a **school**.
:::
