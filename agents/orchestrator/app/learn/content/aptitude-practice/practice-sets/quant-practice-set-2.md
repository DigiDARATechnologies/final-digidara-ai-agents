Target: 18 minutes.

:::example Q1. Successive discount
A shop gives successive discounts of 20% and 10%. What is the single equivalent discount?

**Solution.** Price factor = 0.8 x 0.9 = 0.72. Discount = **28%**.
:::

:::example Q2. Compound interest
Find the compound interest on 10,000 for 2 years at 10% per year.

**Solution.** Amount = 10000 x 1.1 x 1.1 = 12,100. CI = **2,100**.
:::

:::example Q3. Mixture
A 30 litre mixture has milk and water in the ratio 7 : 3. How much water should be added to make the ratio 3 : 2?

**Solution.** Milk = 21 L, water = 9 L. For 3 : 2 with milk 21, water must be 14 L. Add **5 litres**.
:::

:::example Q4. Pipes
Pipe A fills a tank in 12 hours and pipe B empties it in 18 hours. With both open, how long to fill the empty tank?

**Solution.** 1/12 - 1/18 = 3/36 - 2/36 = 1/36. So **36 hours**.
:::

:::example Q5. Ages
A father is 40 and his son is 10. After how many years will the father be twice as old as the son?

**Solution.** 40 + x = 2(10 + x), so x = **20 years**.
:::

:::example Q6. Progression
Find the sum of the first 10 terms of the AP 2, 5, 8, ...

**Solution.** a = 2, d = 3. Sum = 10 / 2 x [2 x 2 + 9 x 3] = 5 x 31 = **155**.
:::

:::example Q7. Circle
The circumference of a circle is 44 cm. Find its area (use pi = 22/7).

**Solution.** 2 x 22/7 x r = 44, so r = 7. Area = 22/7 x 49 = **154 cm squared**.
:::

:::example Q8. Arrangements
How many different arrangements of the letters of the word BANANA are there?

**Solution.** 6 letters with A three times and N twice: 6! / (3! x 2!) = 720 / 12 = **60**.
:::

:::example Q9. Equations
If 2x + 3y = 12 and x - y = 1, find x and y.

**Solution.** x = y + 1, so 2y + 2 + 3y = 12, 5y = 10, y = 2 and **x = 3**.
:::

:::example Q10. Clock
Find the angle between the hands of a clock at 6:30.

**Solution.** |30 x 6 - 5.5 x 30| = |180 - 165| = **15 degrees**.
:::
