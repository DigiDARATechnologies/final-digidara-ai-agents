Try each question first, then read the solution. Target: 15 minutes.

:::example Q1. Percentage
What is 20% of 40% of 500?

**Solution.** 40% of 500 = 200. 20% of 200 = **40**.
:::

:::example Q2. Ratio
Two numbers are in the ratio 3 : 5 and their sum is 80. Find the numbers.

**Solution.** 8 parts = 80, so one part = 10. The numbers are **30 and 50**.
:::

:::example Q3. Profit
An article bought for 800 is sold for 920. What is the profit percent?

**Solution.** Profit = 120. 120 / 800 x 100 = **15%**.
:::

:::example Q4. Simple interest
Find the simple interest on 5,000 at 8% per year for 3 years.

**Solution.** SI = 5000 x 8 x 3 / 100 = **1,200**.
:::

:::example Q5. Work
A can do a job in 10 days and B in 15 days. How long will they take together?

**Solution.** 1/10 + 1/15 = 5/30 = 1/6, so **6 days**.
:::

:::example Q6. Trains
A 120 m long train runs at 54 km/h. How long does it take to cross a pole?

**Solution.** 54 km/h = 15 m/s. Time = 120 / 15 = **8 seconds**.
:::

:::example Q7. Average
The average of 5 numbers is 24. When one number, 36, is removed, what is the average of the remaining four?

**Solution.** Total = 120. Remaining sum = 84. Average = 84 / 4 = **21**.
:::

:::example Q8. LCM
Find the LCM of 12, 18 and 24.

**Solution.** 12 = 2^2 x 3, 18 = 2 x 3^2, 24 = 2^3 x 3. LCM = 2^3 x 3^2 = **72**.
:::

:::example Q9. Boats
A boat's speed in still water is 9 km/h and the stream speed is 3 km/h. How long does it take to go 24 km downstream?

**Solution.** Downstream speed = 12 km/h. Time = 24 / 12 = **2 hours**.
:::

:::example Q10. Probability
Two cards are drawn one after the other without replacement from a pack of 52. What is the probability that both are aces?

**Solution.** 4/52 x 3/51 = 12/2652 = **1/221**.
:::
