---
title: Verbal ability practice set
summary: Eleven verbal questions with explanations: errors, vocabulary, idioms, prepositions, voice, speech and spelling.
takeaways:
- Read the whole sentence before choosing
- Give a reason for each answer; it builds your rules
- Note the grammar points you missed and revise them
---
Target: 10 minutes.

:::example Q1. Find the error
"Each of the boys have finished his work."

**Solution.** "Each" takes a singular verb: "Each of the boys **has** finished his work."
:::

:::example Q2. Synonym
Choose the word closest in meaning to CANDID: (a) secretive (b) frank (c) rude (d) shy

**Solution.** **(b) frank**.
:::

:::example Q3. Antonym
Choose the opposite of FRUGAL: (a) thrifty (b) careful (c) extravagant (d) poor

**Solution.** **(c) extravagant** (wasteful with money).
:::

:::example Q4. Preposition
She is good ___ mathematics. (a) in (b) at (c) on (d) with

**Solution.** **(b) at** ("good at").
:::

:::example Q5. Idiom
"Once in a blue moon" means: (a) frequently (b) never (c) very rarely (d) at night

**Solution.** **(c) very rarely**.
:::

:::example Q6. One word
A person who walks in sleep is called: (a) insomniac (b) somnambulist (c) hypnotist (d) lunatic

**Solution.** **(b) somnambulist**.
:::

:::example Q7. Passive voice
Change to passive: "They are building a bridge."

**Solution.** "A bridge **is being built** by them."
:::

:::example Q8. Reported speech
He said, "I am busy." Change to indirect speech.

**Solution.** He said that **he was busy**.
:::

:::example Q9. Agreement
Neither of the answers ___ correct. (a) are (b) is (c) were (d) have been

**Solution.** "Neither" is singular: **(b) is**.
:::

:::example Q10. Confused words
The ___ of the college addressed the students. (a) principle (b) principal

**Solution.** The head of a college is the **principal**. (A principle is a rule.)
:::

:::example Q11. Spelling
Which is correct? (a) accomodation (b) accommodation (c) acommodation

**Solution.** **(b) accommodation** (double c, double m).
:::
