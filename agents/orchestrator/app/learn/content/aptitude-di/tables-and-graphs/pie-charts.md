## The basics

A pie chart shows how a **whole** is divided into parts. All parts add up to **100%**, which is **360 degrees**.

:::formula
Angle = percentage x 3.6   (or  part / total x 360)
Percentage = angle / 360 x 100
Amount = percentage x total / 100
:::

## Worked example

Monthly budget of a family: total **60,000 rupees**.

| Item | Percent | Angle | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rent | 30% | 108 degrees | 18,000 |
| Food | 25% | 90 degrees | 15,000 |
| Education | 20% | 72 degrees | 12,000 |
| Transport | 15% | 54 degrees | 9,000 |
| Savings | 10% | 36 degrees | 6,000 |
| **Total** | 100% | 360 degrees | 60,000 |

:::example Amount from percentage
How much is spent on food?

**Solution.** 25% of 60,000 = **15,000 rupees**.
:::

:::example Difference
How much more is spent on rent than on transport?

**Solution.** (30% - 15%) of 60,000 = 15% of 60,000 = **9,000 rupees**.
:::

:::example From the angle
In a pie chart, the angle for education is 72 degrees. What percent is it?

**Solution.** 72 / 360 x 100 = **20%**.
:::

:::example Combined parts
What percent is spent on food and transport together, and what is their combined angle?

**Solution.** 25% + 15% = **40%**, and 40 x 3.6 = **144 degrees**.
:::

## Two pie charts

Be careful: the **same percentage** in two charts is **not** the same amount if the totals differ.

:::example Different totals
In 2023 a company's total sales were 400 crore, and 30% came from Product A. In 2024 the total was 500 crore, and 28% came from Product A. Did Product A's sales rise or fall?

**Solution.** 2023: 30% of 400 = 120 crore. 2024: 28% of 500 = 140 crore. The sales **rose by 20 crore**, even though the percentage share fell.
:::

## Reading tips

- Start from the **total** the question gives; if it gives an angle, convert to percent first.
- If percentages are labelled on the chart, you never need the angle.
- If the percentages do not add to 100, one part is "others" or missing.

:::trick
To find an amount from an angle quickly: amount = angle / 360 x total. For 90 degrees and a total of 60,000, a quarter of 60,000 is 15,000.
:::
