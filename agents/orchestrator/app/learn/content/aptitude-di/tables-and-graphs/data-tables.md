## Before you calculate

1. Read the **title** and the **units** (rupees, crore, thousands, percent). Mixing units is the commonest mistake.
2. Read the **row and column headings**.
3. Read the **question** and find only the cells you need.

## The five question types

:::formula
Percentage = (part / whole) x 100
Percentage growth = (new - old) / old x 100
Average = sum / number of items
Ratio of A to B = A : B (simplify it)
Total = sum of the cells
:::

## Worked table

Revenue of a company by region (in rupees crore):

| Region | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| North | 120 | 150 | 180 |
| South | 100 | 130 | 156 |
| East | 80 | 90 | 108 |
| West | 60 | 80 | 96 |

:::example Total
What was the total revenue in 2024?

**Solution.** 180 + 156 + 108 + 96 = **540 crore**. (The totals for 2022 and 2023 are 360 and 450.)
:::

:::example Percentage growth
What was the percentage growth of North between 2022 and 2024?

**Solution.** (180 - 120) / 120 x 100 = **50%**.
:::

:::example Highest growth
Which region had the highest percentage growth from 2022 to 2024?

**Solution.**
- North: 60/120 = 50%
- South: 56/100 = 56%
- East: 28/80 = 35%
- West: 36/60 = 60%

The highest is **West (60%)**, although North had the biggest rise in rupees.
:::

:::example Share
What percentage of the 2023 total revenue came from South?

**Solution.** 130 / 450 x 100 = **28.9%** (approximately).
:::

:::example Average
What was the average annual revenue of East over the three years?

**Solution.** (80 + 90 + 108) / 3 = 278 / 3 = **92.67 crore** (approximately).
:::

:::example Ratio
What is the ratio of North to West revenue in 2024?

**Solution.** 180 : 96. Divide both by 12 to get **15 : 8**.
:::

## Common traps

- **Absolute change vs percentage change**: a big rise in rupees is not always the biggest percentage rise.
- **Percentage vs percentage points**: from 20% to 25% is a rise of 5 percentage points, but a 25% increase.
- **Wrong base**: growth is always divided by the **old** value.
- **Units**: check whether the table is in thousands or lakhs.

:::trick
If the question says "approximately", do not divide exactly. Round to friendly numbers (130 / 450 is about 13 / 45, close to 29%).
:::
