# Flyer brief

- **Size:** A5 portrait (148 x 210 mm), designed in Canva; exported as PNG for social and PDF for print.
- **Print run:** 500 copies, handed out at RS Puram bus stop and two nearby colleges.
- **Must include:** logo, business name, the offer (20% off, code KAAPI20), opening hours,
  address, phone, and a QR code to the landing page with `utm_source=flyer`.
- **One message only:** the offer. Everything else is supporting detail in smaller type.
- **Colours:** coffee brown `#5B3416`, cream `#FFF7EC`, gold `#E3A23B` - the same as the landing page,
  so a visitor who scans the QR code recognises the page straight away.
