Frequently asked questions

What is SkillDARA?

SkillDARA is a platform of eight AI agents that help students and professionals upgrade their skills and become job-ready: coding practice, aptitude training, communication coaching, mock interviews, a resume builder, a job agent, capstone projects and AI certification, all under one account.

Who is SkillDARA for?

Students preparing for placements, fresh graduates, job seekers and working professionals who want to upgrade their skills. Colleges and training institutes can also use it to run assessments and track their learners.

Do I need to pay to start?

No. You can create a free account and get 25 free coins. When you want more usage, you can buy coins in one-time packs inside the app.

How do coins work?

Each agent request uses a few coins. Coins are bought once, never expire, and your balance is shown inside the app. Prices are before GST.

Which AI agents does SkillDARA have?

Coding Practice, Aptitude Trainer, Communication Coach, Mock Interview Agent, Resume Builder, Job Agent, Capstone Project Agent and AI Certification Agent.

Can my college or institute use SkillDARA?

Yes. Organizations get an admin dashboard, staff and class management, assessments with proctoring and reports. Contact support@digidaraaiagents.com and we will set it up.

Is my data safe?

Sessions are secured, agent requests are signed, and privacy controls follow data-protection principles including data export and account deletion.
